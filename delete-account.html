Delete Your Laila Account

Vexiloft · App: Laila (com.vexiloft.laila) · Last updated: August 8, 2026

In short

You can delete your Laila account and all of its data yourself, from inside the app, at any time. It takes about ten seconds and is immediate. If you can no longer sign in, email us and we will do it for you.

Option 1 — Delete it yourself in the app

  1. Open Laila and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings (the menu in the top corner).
  3. Scroll to About.
  4. Tap Delete account.
  5. Type DELETE to confirm, then tap Delete forever.

Your account and data are removed immediately and permanently. You will be signed out and returned to the sign-in screen.

Option 2 — Ask us to delete it

If you have uninstalled the app or can no longer sign in, email vexiloftsupport@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my account".

Send it from the same Google account email you signed in with, so we can confirm the request is really yours. We will confirm and complete the deletion within 30 days.

What gets deleted

DataWhat happens
Your profile (email, name, profile picture)Permanently deleted
All conversations and messages with LailaPermanently deleted
Images you sent to LailaPermanently deleted
Everything Laila remembers about youPermanently deleted
Habits, focus sessions, and app settingsPermanently deleted
Your login itselfPermanently deleted
App-usage and app-blocking dataNever left your device; removed with the app

None of it can be recovered afterwards, and signing up again will not bring any of it back. There is no waiting or "cooling off" period — deletion from inside the app happens straight away.

The one thing we keep

We keep a one-way cryptographic hash of the email address of deleted accounts. A hash cannot be reversed back into your email address and contains no readable personal information.

We keep it for a single purpose: so that deleting an account and signing up again cannot be used to collect repeated free trials. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in preventing abuse of the free tier. Nothing else about you is retained.
